Starting Point - No Turning Back Please

First Blog Post of this incredible journey (Before everything taken by AI)

I have never been writing any blog post and even my writing is quite bad. This will mark my journey through learning game development, which I always have been interested since I was in high school (a long long time ago).

Reason

My reason for doing this is to satisfy my ADHD, else these journeys will finish very quickly, because I have done this many times -> learning new programming languages, learning new engine, learning art, bla bla bla, but I never took it seriously before and I always stop in the middle (worse, it was only starting). I have been thinking about doing these nowadays, but the things that hinder me was the raise of AI which demotivate me, why should I even do this when I can use AI? Will it waste my time?

Procrastination

I have been procrastinating my whole life, should I blame the ADHD or maybe it was just me. I always thought what can I achieve if I took something seriously (since high school you would say new semester new me). This time I hope I can follow all the plan

Motivation

I like creativity. I also like when people enjoy what you make. I used to make a very simple application that I upload to Google Play which luckily can be said somehow successful, and reading all the positives review makes you motivated. Sadly, the procrastination and the feeling that you have imitation syndrome ( I forget what it is called...) made me stop updating it. The other thing is recently my friend visit Gamescom 2026, she shared many things there. Knowing many people that has same interest with you and thinking about having chances to sharing with them is a good insight.

Money

Who doesn't want money, and also gaining it while doing what you love. Easy money is where I start learning game development more than 10 years ago. Remember Flappy bird? I read the news back then and it gain $500,000 USD per day. Ehem... :) :0 Knowing this I made a game back then, but it never sailed.

Back to Zero, AI?

I am at this time will go back to zero, means learning everything from scratch. I will try as much as possible not using AI (which I hope I won't regret)

Hats, Hats on Sale

Without budget, this guy needs to study everything. I will make a improvement journal about not just programming, I will also do art, also game design. (also try improve my English writing :))

Just have fun?

Wish me Luck

P.S. for me in the next year or two, thanks me later
